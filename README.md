# Mental-health-well-being-data-set-analysis-
Information has been gathered from the 'Kaggle' source data-set on mental health, in attempt to demonstrate gross patients distribution on emotional cooperation, work integration and the impact of family history, mental health history and treatment over working interest, mood swing, coping struggles, extended days indoors, self employment and social weakness.
this study does not reveal treatment methods, or the specific mental illness. 

The methods I used for this EDA are python code, Pands, NumPy, Seaborn, matplotlib and spicy stats.

Though the EDA shows no significant trend regarding how risk factors can effect the presence of a mental status, nor implying wether a subject with mental health issues can sustain a job in corporate, business etc. the findings suggest that treatment could be helpful for work interest, self employment, coping struggles, mood swings and less extend days spent indoors for subjects without a mental health history & family history. However, when they are in present, treatment could be insufficient and there is a need in suggesting an alternative. One such could be to involve the subject in the treatment program, help them learn about the effects of each treatment and encourage them to share their thoughts and experiences. This idea is derived from the "open dialogue paradigm".
It can be concluded however, that lack of treatment may have an adversarial outcome for subjects, as seen in extended days indoors, more social weakness and no work interest. Family & mental history does not correlate explicitly with having a more risk factors shown in this specific study, except for coping struggles, which family & mental history reveal a susceptible  clause for.
