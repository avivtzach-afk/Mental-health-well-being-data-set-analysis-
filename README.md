# Mental-health-well-being-data-set-analysis-
I gathered information from a the 'Kaggle' source data-set on mental health, in attempt to demonstrate gross patients distribution on emotional cooperation, work integration and the impact of family history, mental health history and treatment over working interest, mood swing, coping struggles, extended days indoors, self employment and social weakness.
this study does not reveal treatment methods, or the specific mental illness. 

The methods I used for this EDA are python code, Pands, NumPy, Seaborn, matplotlib and spicy stats.

Though the EDA shows no significant trend regarding how risk factors can effect the presence of a mental status, nor implying wether a subject with mental health issues can sustain a job in corporate, business etc. it can be concluded however, that lack of treatment may have an adversarial outcome for subjects, as seen in extended days indoors, more social weakness and no work interest. Family & mental history does not correlate explicitly with having a more risk factors shown in this specific study, except for coping struggles, which family & mental history reveal a susceptible  clause for.
