# Mental-health-well-being-data-set-analysis-
I gathered information from a the 'Kaggle' source data-set on mental health, in attempt to demonstrate gross patients distribution on emotional cooperation, work integration and the impact of family history, mental health history and treatment over working interest, mood swing, coping struggles, extended days indoors, self employment and social weakness.
this study does not reveal treatment methods, or the specific mental illness.
